FinPilot is an adaptive personal finance engine built for Indian earners across income levels. It looks at your income, expenses, emergency savings and debts, then tells you whether to clear debt, build a safety buffer or start investing. From there it generates a recommendation matched to your risk appetite (Safety First, Balanced Shield or Wealth Accelerator), with exact ₹ amounts per month for each asset.

Features

Debt-vs-invest gatekeeper with a graduated status system (Red, Yellow, Green)
Debt recovery simulator comparing regular and accelerated payoff, with interest saved and a debt-free date
Income-tier-aware recommendations, from micro-SIPs at ₹30k to tax-saving options at ₹80k and above
Risk-matched financial news radar with plain-English “What this means for your money” notes
Interactive Chart.js allocation breakdown and a 60-month debt and wealth projection
Save, reset and export your plan locally

Privacy: everything runs in your browser and your numbers never leave your device. Just open index.html.

Disclaimer: FinPilot is an educational tool, not SEBI-registered investment advice. Returns shown are assumptions, not guarantees.
